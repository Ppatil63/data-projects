# Insights — Support Ticket SLA Analytics

> **Data honesty note:** the 3,000 tickets in this project are *synthetic
> sample data* generated by `generate_data.py` (seeded, reproducible) to
> demonstrate the pipeline. The numbers below are really computed from that
> dataset by `etl.py` / `analysis.sql` — they describe the sample data, not
> a real company's helpdesk.

- **Overall SLA breach rate is 26.96%** — 707 of 2,622 resolved tickets
  missed their SLA target, and 378 tickets (12.6%) were still open at the
  Sep 2, 2026 snapshot.
- **Urgent tickets breach the most.** P1 tickets breached 41.1% of the time
  (average resolution 4.7h against a 4h target) and P2s breached 42.8%
  (10.9h average vs an 8h target), while P4s breached only 8.8%. The tighter
  the clock, the more often the team misses it — a triage/staffing signal,
  not an effort signal.
- **Two categories cause most of the pain.** Software Bug tickets breached
  70.7% (42.1h average resolution) and Integration / API tickets breached
  69.9% (41.6h average). Together they are 785 of 3,000 tickets but the
  clear bottleneck.
- **The L2 queue is where SLAs go to die.** App Support L2 breached 54.5%
  of its resolved tickets, versus 5.7% for Service Desk L1 and 4.5% for
  Infrastructure. L2 handles the bug/integration/data work — exactly the
  categories above.
- **The open backlog is stale, not fresh.** 328 of the 378 open tickets
  (86.8%) were already 15+ days old at the snapshot. That is a backlog
  hygiene problem: a weekly aging review would catch these before customers
  do.
- **Weekend-created tickets resolve 54% slower** (22.8h average for
  Fri–Sun creations vs 14.8h Mon–Thu) — consistent with thinner weekend
  coverage.
- **Quick wins exist at the top of the funnel.** Password Reset (496) and
  Access / Login (482) tickets are 32.6% of all volume, resolve in 0.8h and
  3.3h on average, and never breach — prime candidates for self-service /
  automation deflection.
- **Reopen rate is 7.80%** — roughly 1 in 13 resolved tickets comes back,
  a quality signal worth tracking alongside breach rate.
